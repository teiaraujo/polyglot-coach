# Phase 1 — Pedagogical Intelligence

## Product decisions

- Lesson evaluation: automatic at the end of every lesson.
- Level assessment: both continuous CEFR estimation and an optional dedicated placement test.
- Language tracking: independent profiles for English and Spanish.
- Lesson duration: learner chooses 15, 30, 40, 50, or 60 minutes, or open-ended.

## Automatic lesson review

Every completed lesson produces a Portuguese review with: conversation summary; strengths supported by examples; observed improvement opportunities; original-to-improved phrase examples; vocabulary and expressions; recurring patterns distinguished from one-off slips; CEFR estimate with rationale and confidence; skills not assessed in the session; and one or two actionable goals for the next lesson.

The review must not invent errors or achievements. Comparisons with prior sessions are permitted only when reliable prior evidence for the same language is available.

## CEFR assessment

The tutor continuously adapts to observed ability and may run a dedicated placement test. It considers vocabulary, grammar, sentence complexity, coherence, comprehension demonstrated in the interaction, and the ability to sustain and defend ideas. Results are indicative and are not official certification. English and Spanish estimates must never be conflated.

Text-only interaction cannot establish pronunciation, listening comprehension, accent, or spontaneous spoken fluency. These dimensions are marked as not assessed until suitable audio/voice evidence exists.

## Lesson duration and conversation depth

The tutor asks for a duration before a planned lesson unless the learner has already supplied one. Pacing is flexible (approximately 15% warm-up, 55% conversation, 20% focused development, 10% review). The tutor must explore interesting answers with natural follow-ups and must not close after the opening answer. The learner may end early or extend the lesson.

Text chat may not expose reliable elapsed time. Duration is therefore a target, not an exact timer; the tutor must not claim precise elapsed time without a reliable timing capability.

## Independent language profiles

English and Spanish maintain separate CEFR estimates, vocabulary, recurring errors, reviews, and goals. In the absence of durable storage, tracking is limited to the current conversation and other genuinely available context. The tutor must not imply a permanent cross-chat history or database where none exists.

## Out of scope for Phase 1

- Persistent longitudinal storage and monthly reports (Phase 4).
- Full pronunciation and listening assessment (Phase 3 and requires audio-capable interaction).
- Weekly adaptive plans and expanded professional learning paths (Phase 2).
